Research

My interests lie in Euclidean harmonic analysis, in particular the web of problems connected to the Kakeya conjecture.

Fourier analysis @200

  • Fourier Analysis @ 200
  • Young Researchers Symposium

    A one-week workshop, Fourier Analysis @200, was held 27 June - 1 July 2022.

    The workshop was hosted by the ICMS in Edinburgh, Scotland, and marks the 200th anniversary of the publication of Fourier's treatise Théorie analytique de la chaleur. The aim was to bring together experts in harmonic analysis with interests in its many exciting (and expanding) interfaces with other areas of mathematics.

    The workshop also took the opportunity to celebrate the 60th birthday of Jim Wright and his many contributions to modern Fourier analysis.

    As a satellite event there was a 2-day Young Researchers Symposium at the ICMS in Edinburgh,   23rd June - 24th June 2022.
